# Dataset notes

Use only datasets you are legally permitted to access. FaceForensics++, Celeb-DF and DFDC have different access and licensing conditions. ASVspoof data are subject to the challenge terms. Do not place victim/private material in sample_data.

Expected image layout:
`datasets/image/real/*` and `datasets/image/fake/*`.

Expected audio layout:
`datasets/audio/human/*` and `datasets/audio/ai/*`.

NLP CSV requires `text,threat,blackmail,sextortion,financial_coercion`.
